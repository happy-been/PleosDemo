# Courtyard visual prototype assets

All third-party scans below are from Poly Haven, licensed [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Asset | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Asphalt 03 | https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_03 | Scanned ground colour, normal, and roughness/AO |
| Concrete Floor | https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor | Curbs, walkways, apartment facade variation |
| Brown Planks 03 | https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_planks_03 | Bench timber |
| Covered Car | https://polyhaven.com/a/covered_car | Parked car scans; these are not Grandeur models |
| Shrub 01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_01 | Planting bed foliage |
| Tree Small 02 | https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02 | Courtyard trees |
| Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Puresky | https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky | Sky lighting and reflections |

Original download URLs, hashes, and sizes are recorded in `AssetSources.json`. Mesh copies were simplified in Blender for this prototype. Packed metallic/AO/smoothness textures, foliage opacity maps, and neutralized concrete/plaster colour maps are derived from the supplied channels. Leaf opacity is applied to diffuse PNGs during preparation, so those derived files may differ from the recorded original download hash. The road's metre-scaled paint, wear, cracks, and stains were authored specifically for PARKSIDE. Apartment buildings, curbs, wheel stops, grates, bollards, and the bench are project-authored geometry.

The scene is an independent PC visual test. It does not reproduce a specific real apartment site or claim calibrated vehicle behaviour. Its rendering cost has not been measured on Pleos AVD.
